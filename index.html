    How do I get my Google Maps lists?
    1. Saved lists (Want to go, Favorites, your own lists): open Google Takeout → Deselect all → tick Saved → Next step → Create export. Unzip the download: every list is a .csv file.
    2. Starred / labeled places: in Takeout tick Maps (your places) instead and use Saved Places.json.
    3. Google My Maps: open the map → ⋮ menu → Export to KML/KMZ → tick Export as KML.
    4. Just a few places? In Google Maps tap Share → Copy link, open the link in your browser, then paste the full address from the address bar below. Plain place names work too.
